# Verified Resources Policy

## Purpose

The Supply Chest is Lanternmere's home for useful resources and guides. It may link to verified external sites so Lanternmere can be a one-stop operational hub without duplicating specialist tools.

"Verified" means Lanternmere has deliberately reviewed the destination and identified the canonical/official service or trusted resource. It does not mean Lanternmere guarantees third-party content, availability, security, or advice.

## Resource principles

- Prefer official Blizzard resources and canonical provider domains.
- For third-party tools, link to the provider's canonical site rather than mirrors, copied guides, or unverified redirects.
- Clearly identify the provider and what Lanternmere uses or recommends the link for.
- Keep integration status separate from link status: a verified link does not imply an API integration.
- Open external destinations safely and make it obvious that the user is leaving Lanternmere.
- Review important destinations periodically and remove dead, redirected, compromised, or materially changed resources.
- Do not copy restricted third-party content into Lanternmere merely because a link is approved.
- Preserve required attribution for integrated providers.

## Initial categories

- Blizzard / World of Warcraft official resources.
- Raider.IO.
- Warcraft Logs.
- Raidbots.
- Encounter and strategy resources.
- Class/spec resources.
- Mythic+ route/resources.
- Profession/crafting resources.

Specific destinations should be added only after their canonical URLs and current suitability are verified.
