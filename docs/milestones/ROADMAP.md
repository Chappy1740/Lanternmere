# Lanternmere Product Roadmap

## Current

- Milestones 0–6 — implemented.
- Next planned milestone: Milestone 7 — The Guild Hall: Guild Operations.

## Guild Operations era

- Milestone 7 — The Guild Hall: Guild Operations — first-class guild foundation, membership, permissions, roster, raid/readiness foundations, attendance, loot history, settings, and multi-guild architecture.
- Milestone 8 — The Raid Room: Raid Operations — Raid Mode, smart roster support, encounters, assignments, attendance, and operational Loot Council.
- Milestone 9 — The War Table: Weekly Command Center — personalized weekly priorities, leadership briefing, availability, reset awareness, and guild calendar.
- Milestone 10 — The Muster: Recruitment & Trials — recruitment needs, applicants, trials, officer workflow, and decision history.
- Milestone 11 — The Artisan Hall: Professions & Guild Services — professions, crafting discovery, crafting requests, and guild resource goals.
- Milestone 12 — The Expedition Board: Mythic+ Operations — guild key groups, role matching, weekly goals, Vault context, and key-night planning.
- Milestone 13 — The Chronicle Lens: Progression Intelligence — Warcraft Logs, Raider.IO, Raidbots handoffs, trends, and post-raid review.
- Milestone 14 — The Lanternkeeper: Assisted Guild Intelligence — permission-aware summaries and operational briefings built from Lanternmere's authorized data. Architecture is defined early in `docs/lanternkeeper-ai-architecture.md` so Milestones 7–13 remain AI-ready without making AI a dependency.

## Cross-cutting product direction

- The Hearth remains the personalized entry point.
- Guilds are first-class and multi-guild capable.
- The Supply Chest is the verified-resource hub for specialist sites and guides.
- External integrations use supported APIs or explicit handoffs, cached snapshots, attribution, timestamps, consent, and failure/stale states.
- Lanternmere organizes and connects specialist information; it does not need to replace every specialist website.
- Leadership decisions remain human-controlled and auditable.
- Mobile/tablet/desktop usability, accessibility, privacy, and RLS remain product requirements.
